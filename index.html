Final decision

Scale the fleet, but operate it by rhythm.

Demand growth is real and statistically decisive. Capacity should expand for the higher 2012 baseline, while daily execution follows three reliable signals: commuter peaks, seasonal temperature comfort, and adverse-weather suppression. Use Random Forest as a planning aid—not as a substitute for the evidence.
